Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

The short version

Deal Logger does not collect, sell or share any of your data. The deals you log stay in your own browser, on your own computer. We never see them.

Who we are

Deal Logger is a free browser extension for Chrome and Firefox, made by Yafet Segued. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean the maker of Deal Logger.

What the extension stores

When you log a deal, the extension saves what you type so it can show your totals and charts:

  • Customer name, vehicle, stock number and sale date
  • Deal type (finance, cash, lease or outside finance), new or used, and full or half deal
  • Commission, if you choose to enter it
  • Your monthly goal and your last-used deal type

All of this is saved only in your browser’s local storage on your device. It is never sent to us, to a server, or to anyone else. The extension makes no network requests.

What we don’t do

  • No accounts and no sign-in
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising
  • No selling, renting or sharing of data — we don’t have any to share
  • No reading of the web pages you visit or your browsing history

Permissions

Deal Logger asks your browser for one permission: storage, so it can keep your deals on your device. It does not ask to read or change websites, and it cannot see what’s in your tabs.

Customer information

Because the deals you log can include customer names, please follow your dealership’s rules for handling customer information. Deal Logger keeps that information on your computer only, but you are in control of what you enter and who can use your computer.

Deleting your data

You can delete any deal from the Car Sales Tracker at any time. Removing the extension, or clearing your browser’s site data, also erases everything it stored. Since we never receive a copy, we can’t recover deleted data — print your monthly report if you want a backup.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log basic technical information (such as your IP address) for security and reliability; see GitHub’s Privacy Statement for details. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts. We don’t use cookies, analytics or ads on this site.

The live dashboard demo saves sample deals only in your own browser, the same way the extension does.

Children

Deal Logger is made for working car sales professionals and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the “Last updated” date above.
